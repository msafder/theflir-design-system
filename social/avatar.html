At 32px no wordmark is legible. That is true of every mark at that size and is not a fault to design around — the size that has to work is 150.

Rules

The mark is the full lockup, never cropped and never restacked. The stacked construction on the suture8-01 cover would fit a circle far better and is forbidden here — GDL-05 records it as historical, correct for 2002 and wrong for new work. "FLIR" alone is never permissible, at any size, for any reason.

Set at ~68% of frame width. A first pass at 51% proofed clean against the circle but read weakly at the sizes an avatar is actually seen at. Legibility at thumbnail size is the whole job here, and the clear space left over is still comfortable.

Same instrument field as every other surface — grime, hard grain, scanline and vignette, all under the type. No photograph: at 150px a photographic field turns to mud and the mark has to carry alone.

Render with ?guides=1 to prove the crop. That overlay is a proof layer and must never appear in a shipped export.

Render

cd theflir-design-system python3 -m http.server 8893 "/Applications/Google Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google Chrome" \ --headless --disable-gpu --hide-scrollbars \ --force-device-scale-factor=1 --window-size=1080,1080 \ --virtual-time-budget=4000 --screenshot=avatar.png \ http://127.0.0.1:8893/social/avatar.export.html

Deviation · http, not file:// Serve over http. From file:// the local @font-face TTFs are blocked, Placard Condensed and Frankfurter fall back to Impact, and the wordmark ships wrong. The render still succeeds — the failure is silent.
Deviation · scale factor 1 --force-device-scale-factor=1 is required on a Retina Mac, or Chrome writes 2160×2160. Harmless for an avatar, which gets downscaled anyway, but keep the master at its declared size.
