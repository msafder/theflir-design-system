What theflir.com looks like pasted into iMessage, Slack, WhatsApp or a Facebook post — for most people the first THE FLIR surface they ever see, and the only one they see without choosing to. Wordmark, one line of orientation, credit line. Filed under theflir.com rather than Instagram: it is the site's preview and is never posted to a feed.

Rules

The artwork lives in social/og-card.export.html — a bare 1200×630 page with no card chrome, embedded above. This card documents it and never re-draws it, so the preview and the shipped PNG cannot drift apart.

GDL-07 stack in order: image, crush, hard grain, scanline, then type. The grain layer stops under the type layer.

The field is flirbackground.jpg, already lit red, so it takes --flir-photo-filter-native rather than the grayscale pass. That field is the one red on this surface — the type is white and grey, and nothing else here may be red.

The wordmark is .flir-wordmark, never re-typed by hand. Its locked 1:2 construction only holds if both local faces load, which is why this renders over http and not file:// — see the deviation opposite.

Type sits left, over the flat part of the field; the branches on the right stay clear of it. Some layouts crop the edges of a 1.91:1 card, so nothing that must be read comes within 72px of an edge.

No release title, no date, no tour. A card that names a record has to be re-rendered the day that record stops being current, and it never is.

Render

cd theflir-design-system python3 -m http.server 8898 "/Applications/Google Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google Chrome" \ --headless --disable-gpu --hide-scrollbars \ --force-device-scale-factor=1 --window-size=1200,630 \ --virtual-time-budget=4000 --screenshot=og.png \ http://127.0.0.1:8898/social/og-card.export.html

Deviation · http, not file:// The export page must be served over http. From file://, the local @font-face TTFs are blocked, Placard Condensed and Frankfurter both fall back to Impact, and the wordmark ships wrong at the one size where it is most visible. The failure is silent — the render succeeds and merely looks off.
Deviation · scale factor 1 --force-device-scale-factor=1 is not optional on a Retina Mac. Without it Chrome renders at 2x and writes a 2400×1260 file that no longer matches the declared og:image dimensions.

Ships to theflir.com/public/images/og.png, declared in that repo's public/index.html as an absolute URL — relative og:image paths are not resolved by Facebook, iMessage or Slack.
