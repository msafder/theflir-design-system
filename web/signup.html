Rules

The submit control is the one red fill on the surface. Where the page already spends its red — a current release row, a ticket link — this button drops to an --flir-accent outline on transparent and the red stays where it was.

The field label is visible, mono, uppercase. A placeholder is not a label: it vanishes on focus and leaves the field unnamed for anyone using a screen reader.

Square. --flir-radius-hair on the input is the one radius GDL-03 grants to inputs; the button and the block are --flir-radius-none. A pill button here is a GDL-03 violation — the pill is legal only on the NOW PLAYING chip.

Copy is GDL-04 flat: what arrives, and how often. No incentive, no "join the family", no emoji. The confirmation sentence is not optional — the list is double opt-in, and the visitor is told that before submitting rather than after.

The status line always occupies its line, empty or not. A message that appears out of nothing shoves the credit line down and reflows the page under the reader's thumb.

One block per surface. Two capture forms on one page compete and both convert worse.

Binding Klaviyo, account Snb8Wc, list STGBxc "THE FLIR" — deliberately separate from the label-side store lists. Same account, distinct list and tags. The client subscription endpoint takes the email property only: adding a subscriptions block to the profile payload returns 400.
