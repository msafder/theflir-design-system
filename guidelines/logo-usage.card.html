GDL-05 · Placard Condensed + Frankfurter Heavy

The wordmark already exists and is not being redrawn. It is typed as one word — THEFLIR — in two faces at a fixed 1:2 ratio:

THEPlacard Condensed · 1×
FLIRFrankfurter Heavy · 2×
BaselineShared · no shift
Word spaceNone · typed as one word
TrackingFont default · no adjustment
Worked exampleTHE 75pt / FLIR 150pt

The article is part of the name. The band is never referred to as "FLIR" alone — that is an unrelated business. Both words always appear together, on one line, at this ratio.

In CSS the parent's font-size is the FLIR size; THE follows from --flir-wordmark-ratio. Set one number and the lockup scales correctly.
