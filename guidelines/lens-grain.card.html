Lens & Grain

GDL-07

The band named Anton Corbijn as the reference, and specifically two things: the high-contrast grain and the wide-angle distortion. Those are two different decisions made at two different moments. Distortion happens at the camera and cannot be added afterwards. Grain happens in the render and is a token.

At the camera — wide angle
Focal length

24–35mm full-frame. Below 24mm the distortion stops reading as a point of view and starts reading as a mistake.

Distance

Close. The wide lens only distorts if the subject is inside a metre or so — a 28mm shot from across the room is just a wide shot.

Verticals

Let them converge. Tilt the camera and keep the keystoning. Do not correct it in post.

Horizon

High or absent. The subject sits low in a large field of sky, wall or black.

Edges

Stretched limbs and hands at the frame edge are the point, not a flaw. Frame so something is being pulled.

Framing

Off-centre and loose. Leave the dead space; it is where the type goes.

In the render — grain
Filter

--flir-photo-filter-corbijn — grayscale, contrast 1.42, brightness 0.90. Blacks crush, midtones pull apart, highlights hold just short of blowing.

Grain

--flir-grain-hard — 2px, roughly three times the density of the default. At this weight grain is a visible material, not a texture hint.

Order

Filter on the image, grain in a sibling layer above it, type above that. Grain never sits over type and never touches the mark.

Where

Hero fields on theflir.com, the gig poster, the CD jacket front. Not on Instagram feed posts — the platform's own compression fights it and the result reads as artefacting.

Limit of the mediumWide-angle distortion is a shooting decision. There is no token for it and no CSS that produces it honestly — a transform applied to an undistorted photograph looks like a transform. When commissioning or selecting photography, the specification above is the deliverable; the system only carries the grain half.
