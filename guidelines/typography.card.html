Four faces, two of them the band's own. Placard Condensed and Frankfurter Heavy build the wordmark; Placard also sets every headline at 48px and up. Archivo carries UI-scale titles and body copy. IBM Plex Mono carries anything that reads as instrument output — track lists, timestamps, catalogue numbers, credits, micro-labels. Uppercase is always tracked; lowercase never is.
